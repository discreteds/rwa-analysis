# Extraction and validation principles

| Principle | Status | Summary |
|---|---|---|
| [Source-led extraction and golden sets](source-led-extraction-and-golden-sets.md) | AGREED | Verify faithful capture against independent source expectations, keeping research limitations separate from extraction errors. |
| [Validation is a separate process](validation-is-a-separate-process.md) | AGREED | Validation is not embedded in processing; its scope and behaviour remain undefined and must not be invented during implementation. |
