# Architecture principles

| Principle | Status | Summary |
|---|---|---|
| [Capture and tag for research](capture-and-tag-for-research.md) | AGREED | Faithful capture and useful tagging support assumption-aware, approximate research without requiring perfect accounting. |
| [Evidence and interpretation](evidence-and-interpretation.md) | AGREED | Preserve source evidence and distinguish reported facts from interpretations and derivations. |
| [Multi-domain source bundles](multi-domain-source-bundles.md) | AGREED | One file's extraction can span domains, portfolios, periods and jurisdictions. |
| [Rebuildable analytical layers](rebuildable-analytical-layers.md) | AGREED | Full Silver regeneration is the primary workflow; durable inputs reproduce derived outputs. |
| [Composed processing and organisation staging](composed-processing-and-organisation-staging.md) | AGREED | Separate configuration, declarations, operations and workflow; organisation `run()` sessions and independent staging feed a full master rebuild. |
| [Emergent analysis and selective persistence](emergent-analysis-and-selective-persistence.md) | AGREED | Persist durable knowledge; compute exploratory relationships on demand and retain useful outputs selectively. |
