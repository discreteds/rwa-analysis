## Sources and reading convention

Sources are the retained official reports in [raw/index.md](../../raw/index.md):
CBA 30 June 2025 (118 PDF pages), Westpac 30 September 2025 (102), NAB
30 September 2025 (99), ANZ 30 September 2025 (122).

All locations below are **PDF pages**. CBA, Westpac and ANZ printed page numbers
match these locations; NAB printed pages are PDF page minus two. Ranges include
continuations, comparative tables and associated explanations. Each bank column
also serves as its document inventory; related panels are grouped rather than
counted as separate disclosures.

Reviewed page-by-page extracted-text outlines across all four reports and full text
for the highlighted comparisons, NAB's disclosure index and ANZ's modifications.
Mortgage CR9 layouts were visually reviewed in the preceding evidence investigation.
This is a navigable table-family inventory, not a claim that every cell, footnote or
embedded panel has received detailed semantic review. Exact per-panel titles,
comparative dates, units, precision and all scope exceptions remain to be expanded
before extraction. A missing location means **not located**, unless a source gives
an explicit omission or external reference.

Explicit exceptions matter:

- **NAB CR7:** p.95 says no credit derivatives held for credit mitigation at either
  current or previous period end. This is an explained omission, not missing research data.
- **NAB IRRBB1:** p.96 says the new template is not disclosed because revised APS 117
  took effect after September 2025. The old-regime shock table still exists on p.83.
- **ANZ IRRBB1:** pp.102/117 explicitly replace the new template with previous APS 330
  information. Its heading alone must not imply the new scenario specification.
- **Westpac CCR:** p.60 limits these tables to Australian exposures; NZ-regulated
  CCR is reported in the RBNZ credit-risk asset class. Other jurisdictions are described
  as immaterial, with $713m RWA. Do not infer the missing CCR3 represents zero.
- **External disclosure versus absent:** ANZ CCA p.25 and Westpac G-SIB p.86 point
  outside the report. Acquisition is follow-up; no external values have been captured.
- **NAB requirements index:** pp.94–96 separately labels non-required templates
  (including PV1, REM, CCR7 and certain market/CVA templates) and G-SIB-only TLAC

## Comparison findings

### 1. CR6–CR9: similar bands, different observation contexts

The mortgage blocks use the same seven non-default band labels plus default. That
permits a band-label comparison, not an automatic same-cohort join.

| Bank | Mortgage CR9 | Annual outcome window | Historical rate history | Material qualification |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| CBA | 60 | Apr 2024–Mar 2025 | From 2014 | Grade midpoints determine disclosure bands; history uses asset classes under standards at the time; CR6 is June 2025 |
| Westpac | 55; notes 56 | Jul 2024–Jun 2025 | Ten years | PD and EAD weights anchored at June 2024; new-entrant defaults separately shown; CR6 is September 2025 |
| NAB | 61; definitions 60 | Financial year to Sep 2025 | Ten years | Weighted PD at reporting date; default counted if non-performing at any month-end, including later cures/exits; mortgage counting follows CR6 agreements |
| ANZ | 66 | Annual counts shown; precise cohort/rating anchor still to establish | Five years for mortgages | Historical numerator excludes technical defaults, denominator excludes exits; corporate 2.5-year exception does not apply to mortgages |

Concrete comparison: Westpac's 2.5–<10% mortgage band reports **7.93% weighted PD**
and **5.80% historical annual defaults** (p.55). The former uses June 2024 EAD/PD;
the latter averages ten years. Their difference is not a measured 2025 calibration
error. CBA's corresponding historical rate is **2.80%** (p.60), but different history,
band allocation and populations prevent interpreting this as twice the credit quality.

CR9 supplies count-based outcomes and arithmetic PD as well as weighted PD. Preserve
these separate measures; EAD-weighted PDB and a realised borrower-count discrimination
measure have different weighting. Opening/closing counts and new-entrant defaults
must be retained before deriving an annual cohort default rate. Band migration rules
need review before calculating a realised Gini.

Verified historical rates and extraction follow-up are retained in the
[mortgage evidence backlog](../04.planning/a.backlog/research/mortgage-distribution-evidence.md).

### 2. A source-backed second connection: NAB CR8 to credit RWA summary

NAB p.59 explicitly distinguishes IRB RWA (column a), other credit RWA (b), and total
credit RWA (c). At September 2025, in $m:

`322,228 + 52,758 = 374,986`

The **374,986** is also the total credit-risk RWA in the overview on p.13. CR8's text
explicitly states this connection. Its other-credit column includes standardised and
slotting credit, CCR and securitisation. This gives a useful recorded relationship
between table measures without assuming every CR8 column describes IRB alone.

The overview also shows **352,908** in its narrower credit-risk RWA column and
**11,731** for CCR, with CVA **4,942** and securitisation **5,405** separately:

`352,908 + 11,731 + 4,942 + 5,405 = 374,986`

These columns are not interchangeable. Nor is CR8's IRB total automatically identical
to the overview's row labelled “Total IRB approach”: the overview separately presents
the RBNZ subsidiary. Further decomposition needs its scope bridge. This example is
an explanatory analytical connection, not a processing reconciliation gate.

### 3. Other consequential differences already visible

- **CRB exposure basis:** NAB's industry table p.37 uses post-CCF/post-CRM EAD,
  while ANZ p.43 uses gross carrying amounts split into loans, off-balance sheet and
  other financial assets. Their mortgage rows are not a directly comparable market-share
  denominator. NAB also separates credit-only from credit-plus-CCR EAD.
- **Ageing grain:** NAB p.36 combines 30–89 days and gives an “of which mortgage”
  column; ANZ p.45 separates 30–59 and 60–89 days with a mortgage row. ANZ can be
  aggregated to that age interval, but this does not resolve EAD versus carrying-value
  differences. Retain the finer source split.
- **CR2 title versus scope:** NAB p.36 says the movements include off-balance sheet
  exposures despite the title mentioning loans and debt securities. Westpac's p.36
  title explicitly includes off-balance sheet. Read definitions, not just titles.
- **VaR:** CBA p.82 uses a ten-day horizon; Westpac p.75 explicitly uses one day.
  Both report period mean/high/low/end, but raw values are not like-for-like. No
  square-root-of-time conversion is justified without an analytical assumption.
- **Operational losses:** CBA p.89 assigns losses by identification date; ANZ p.98
  says financially incurred year and ends its displayed history in September 2024.
  Report cover date and loss-year definition cannot stand in for observation dates.
- **Liquidity:** LCR uses period averages; NSFR uses reporting-date balances and
  regulatory factor-weighted amounts. See the earlier
  [liquidity discovery](2025-liquidity-metadata-discovery.md) for field-level distinctions.
